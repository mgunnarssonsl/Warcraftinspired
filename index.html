Controls

  • Left-click select · drag box-select · Shift add/remove
  • Double-click a unit: select all of that type on screen
  • Right-click ground: move · enemy: attack · mine/tree: gather · Keep: return cargo · unfinished building: resume · damaged building: repair
  • Shift + any order queues it after the current one (waypoints, several buildings for one Laborer)
  • M move · S stop · A attack / attack-move · H hold position · G gather · R return cargo · E repair (workers)
  • After killing the enemy you clicked, soldiers attack the next enemy they can see by themselves (Laborers stop)
  • With a Laborer: F Farm · L Lumber Mill · B Barracks · T Watch Tower · V Hall of Valor · N Blacksmith · K Keep · tier II: C Chapel · W Siege Works. Playing the Thornwild, the same keys plant its matching buildings (F Grove, B Thicket Den, V Elder Circle…), which grow on their own
  • With a building: Q, W, E train or research (Barracks E: Lancer · Keep W: Castle, then Citadel · Keep E: Mule Carts) · right-click sets the rally point · X cancels the last item (or a construction)
  • Heroes (Hall of Valor Q Marshal · W Pathfinder · E Sage; a second hero with the Castle). F2 selects them in turn. Q, W, E cast (learned at levels 1, 2, 3)
  • Items: a hero right-clicks an item on the ground to pick it up · Z, X, C use an inventory slot · right-click a slot to drop it · select a Trading Post and press its keys to buy (a hero must stand next to it)
  • Scouting: the Barracks trains the Outrider (R), a fast scout · with a Keep selected, F fires a Signal Flare at any spot (click the ground or the minimap) · enemy buildings you have seen stay on your map as they were last seen until you look again · the minimap marks camps you have found by level
  • . next idle worker · Space jump to last alert / selection
  • Ctrl+1–9 save group (Mac: Ctrl or ⌥ Option; ⌘+number switches browser tabs) · Shift+1–9 add to group · 1–9 select it (twice to center)
  • Hold and drag the right (or middle) mouse button to grab and move the map · arrows or screen edges scroll · wheel zoom
  • P pause · F10 menu: save, load, settings, restart, surrender · ` debug overlay · F1 this panel
  • The keys above are the defaults: change any of them in Settings › Controls. Settings also hold sound, graphics, camera speed, interface size and colour-blind friendly teams.

Map. Brigand camps come in three levels; wiping one out drops an item (better items from harder camps). Trading Posts sell items to any hero standing beside them, and Healing Fountains heal everyone nearby: both sit halfway between the bases.

Roster. Guards beat Lancers, Lancers beat Archers, Archers beat Guards. Clerics heal on their own; Mangonels crush buildings and clumps but are helpless up close. Hover a train button for each unit's strengths.

Put your Laborers to work: gold from mines (two miners fit inside at once), wood from trees. Resources count only when delivered. Train more Laborers at the Keep, build Farms for population, a Lumber Mill near the forest for shorter wood trips, and a Barracks for soldiers. Brigand camps guard the richer mines: clear them to expand.
