Controls

  • Left-click select · drag box-select · Shift add/remove
  • Double-click a unit: select all of that type on screen
  • Right-click ground: move · enemy: attack · mine/tree: gather · Keep: return cargo · unfinished building: resume
  • M move · S stop · A attack / attack-move · H hold position · G gather · R return cargo
  • With a Laborer: F Farm · L Lumber Mill · B Barracks · T Watch Tower · K Keep, then click to place (Shift: keep placing)
  • With a Keep / Barracks: Q, W train · right-click sets the rally point · X cancels the last unit (or a construction)
  • . next idle worker · Space jump to last alert / selection
  • Ctrl+1–9 set group · 1–9 recall (twice to center)
  • Arrows, screen edges or middle-drag to scroll · wheel zoom
  • P pause · F10 menu (surrender, restart) · ` debug overlay · F1 this panel

Put your Laborers to work: gold from mines (two miners fit inside at once), wood from trees. Resources count only when delivered. Train more Laborers at the Keep, build Farms for population, a Lumber Mill near the forest for shorter wood trips, and a Barracks for soldiers. Brigand camps guard the richer mines: clear them to expand.
